Counts come straight from each state's published results (North Carolina State Board of Elections, California Secretary of State, Washington Secretary of State), checked about every minute while this page is open and every 10 minutes otherwise. A "drop" is any update that changed a race's totals; the record starts from when this page first read each state. Margin is between the top two candidates. Votes left are estimates built from each county's own count of uncounted ballots (in North Carolina, the provisional ballots cast); the projection splits each county's share the way that county has voted so far. Market odds are shown for comparison.
